Common questions

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your device. There is no account. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not transfer between devices.

How do I unlock more heroes?
Open the Hero tab, tap Swap, and unlock a hero with gold or gems you earn by playing.

How do weapons evolve?
Raise a weapon to 5 stars in a run while holding its partner item. The evolution is then offered as an upgrade.

Why do I see ads?
Ads are optional. They only play when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one.

Does the game cost money?
No. There are no purchases in the game.

The game runs slowly.
Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and turn on Lighter effects.
