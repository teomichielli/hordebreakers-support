Privacy Policy

Hordebreakers for iPhone. Last updated 8 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the game handles. The game is made by Teo Michielli, an independent developer.

What the game itself collects

Nothing that identifies you. There is no account, no sign-in and no online profile. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location
  • which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them
  • diagnostic data such as device model, system version and crash information

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

The first time the game runs it asks whether it may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also simply not watch ads: they only play when you tap a reward button.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and sale

I do not sell your data. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
